WHY WE BEGAN

A better pause
in the day.

We wanted to create a coffee house that felt considered, not complicated.

Somewhere the coffee is taken seriously, but never itself. Somewhere a quick espresso can become a slow conversation. Somewhere the room feels generous whether you stay for five minutes or the whole morning.

That is Ember & Oak: exceptional ingredients, honest hospitality and an atmosphere with room to breathe.
